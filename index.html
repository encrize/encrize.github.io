Hey, I'm encrize — an 18-year-old developer and cybersecurity specialist. I'm usually online, building something, taking something apart, or trying to figure out why it broke.

I make web sites, apps, games and low-level software, but most of my time goes into reverse engineering and malware analysis.

Right now I am working on fukOS, my own 32-bit x86 hobby operating system written from scratch in C and Assembly, together with fuk1 — a small interpreted language made to run on top of it.

I like projects that make me understand what is really happening under the hood. No magic, no giant frameworks — just code, mistakes and debugging.
