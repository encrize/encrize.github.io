~/about.txt- □ ✕

hey, i'm encrize - an 18-year-old developer and cybersecurity specialist. i'm usually online, building something, taking something apart, or trying to figure out why it broke.

i make websites, apps, games, and low-level software, but most of my time goes into reverse engineering and malware analysis.

when i'm not tearing software apart, i'm building it. right now i'm working on fukOS, my own 32-bit x86 hobby operating system written from scratch in c and assembly, along with fuk1 - a custom interpreted programming language designed to run on top of it.

i like projects that force me to understand what is actually happening under the hood. no magic, no giant frameworks - just code, mistakes, debugging, and the occasional moment when everything somehow works.

this site is my little corner of the internet, inspired by the old-school 2000s web - back when people built personal websites instead of collecting profiles on big platforms.

feel free to explore my projects below or text me if you want to talk!
